# Privacy policy, what we collect and what we do with it
LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

Omnya Lab® ("we", "us", "our") operates the website omnyalab.com. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our site or interact with our services.

By using our website you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

## 01 What we collect
When you contact us via email, phone, or WhatsApp, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any information you choose to include in your message. When you book a call through Calendly, your name, email, and scheduling preferences are collected by Calendly on our behalf.

When you browse our website, limited technical data is collected automatically, such as browser type, device information and pages visited, in anonymous and aggregated form.

## 02 How we use your information
We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, deliver the services you request, schedule calls, improve our website and user experience, and understand how visitors interact with our content.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

## 03 Third party services
Vercel hosts our website and provides privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics. You can learn more at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Calendly is used for scheduling calls. When you book a meeting, Calendly collects your name, email, and any information you provide during booking. Their privacy policy is available at calendly.com/privacy.

## 04 Cookies
Our website does not use analytics or marketing cookies. Third party services such as Calendly may set their own cookies when you use them.

## 05 Data retention
We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to comply with legal obligations. Email correspondence is retained for the duration of the business relationship and deleted upon request.

## 06 Your rights
Under applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You also have the right to restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to request data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@omnyalab.com.
