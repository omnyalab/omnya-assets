# Privacy policy, what we collect and what we do with it
LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2026

Omnya Lab® ("we", "us", "our") operates the website omnyalab.com. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our site or interact with our services.

By using our website you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

## 01 What we collect
When you contact us via email, phone, or WhatsApp, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any information you choose to include in your message. When you book a call through Calendly, your name, email, and scheduling preferences are collected by Calendly on our behalf.

When you ask for a free blueprint, we collect your first name and email address, the page you signed up from and the date and wording of the consent you gave. When you buy a guide, Stripe collects your payment details; we receive your name, email and what you bought.

When you browse our website, limited technical data is collected automatically, such as browser type, device information and pages visited, in anonymous and aggregated form.

## 02 How we use your information
We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, deliver the services you request, schedule calls, improve our website and user experience, and understand how visitors interact with our content.

We use your email to send you what you asked for. We send news and offers only if you ticked the box that says so, and every one of those emails has a link to unsubscribe in one click.

We never sell or rent your email address or any other personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

## 03 Third party services
Vercel hosts our website and provides privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics. You can learn more at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Stripe processes payments (stripe.com/privacy). Resend delivers our emails (resend.com/legal/privacy-policy). Upstash stores the sign-up list in the EU or US on our behalf.

Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram) provides the Meta Pixel and the Conversions API we use to measure our ads; see the Cookies section and facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Calendly is used for scheduling calls. When you book a meeting, Calendly collects your name, email, and any information you provide during booking. Their privacy policy is available at calendly.com/privacy.

## 04 Cookies
We use one kind of marketing cookie: the Meta Pixel. It loads only if you click Accept on the cookie banner, and it tells Meta which of our pages you visited, if you asked for a free blueprint and if you opened a checkout, so we can measure our ads and show them to people who already know us. If you click Reject, or do nothing, no Meta cookie is set. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings button at the bottom of this page.

When you buy a guide, our server tells Meta that a purchase happened, with its amount and your email address encrypted (hashed), so we can measure which ads bring sales. Meta processes this data as described in its own privacy policy.

Vercel's analytics are cookie-free. Third party services such as Calendly or Stripe may set their own cookies when you use them.

## 05 Data retention
We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to comply with legal obligations. Email correspondence is retained for the duration of the business relationship and deleted upon request. Sign-ups are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

## 06 Your rights
Under applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You also have the right to restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to request data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@omnyalab.com.
